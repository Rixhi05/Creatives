AboutThe short version

Part storyteller, part strategist

I landed my first paying client in 12th grade and never really stopped. That side hustle grew into Scrap Media, the studio I co-founded, and in 2 years it did ₹40 lakh+ in revenue across 100+ clients. I brought in the clients, set the price (and did the haggling), and pulled in a crew of 5–6 editors I trusted when it got busy. The brief, the deadline and the final cut never left my desk.

Making it pretty is the easy part. Before I open a timeline I want to know who's watching, what makes them stop scrolling and what they should feel by the last frame. The story gets built around that, and then I make it beautiful.

Right now I'm a Research & Media Associate at Headout, on Dex, their cinematic AI audio-tour product. I dig up the stories behind places and turn them into films, maps and images a tour can actually use.

AI productBuilt for my own edits

Rix Studioan automated edit chain you direct, frame by frame

Rix Studio is an editing chain that runs itself, with a director's chair at every step. Drop in raw talking-head footage and it does the work that eats an editor's day: preps the footage, transcribes every word, pitches concepts, storyboards, builds the motion design, finds b-roll, scores it, mixes the sound and renders the final cut. You keep the creative hand on every frame, so even someone who has never opened an editor can ship something that looks like a top short-form studio made it. All it needs from you is the direction.

What the chain handles

  • Strategist firstReads the transcript like a creative strategist: the promise, the hook, the strongest line, the weak spots. Then it pitches three directions.
  • Every layer, designedCamera moves, words on screen, b-roll, transitions, sound and music, laid out on a timeline you can drag, draw on or overrule.
  • Frames you can directTwo finished-looking frames per section. Approve them, ask for options, mark "needs changes" or draw exactly where things go.
  • Builds, scores, mixesRenders the motion in Remotion and ffmpeg, generates music, finds b-roll, grades the colour and mixes the sound under the voice.
  • Checks its own workCritiques every frame for safe areas, hierarchy and how it reads on a phone, then verifies loudness, sync and frame count.
  • Gets better every editApproved lessons and taste notes live in its brain, so each project starts where the last one ended.

It runs privately on my Mac, so there's no public link. I'm always happy to demo it live on a call.
