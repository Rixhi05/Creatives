About me

So, who's Rishi?

I started freelancing back in 12th grade and took it seriously from day one. That turned into Scrap Media. 100+ clients later, I still find every client myself, do the pricing (and the haggling), and when things get busy I bring in 5–6 editors I trust. The brief, the deadline and the final cut stay on me.

I care about how a video looks, but I care more about whether it works. Who's watching? What'll make them stop scrolling? I figure that out first, build the story around it, and then make it pretty.

These days I'm a Research & Media Associate at Headout, on Dex. It's their AI audio-tour product (the cinematic kind), and I handle the research and the media side.
